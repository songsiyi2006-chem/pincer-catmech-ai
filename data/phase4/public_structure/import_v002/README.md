# Verified crystal extraction; chemical and quantum review still required

This is the second immutable import, which adds explicit provenance and third-party licensing metadata required by the HPC bridge. The first import remains in the parent directory. Use `Mn1/crystal_candidate.xyz` and `Mn2/crystal_candidate.xyz` from **this `import_v002` directory** for the reviewed prospective job pack.

The source is the published Mn precursor `{MnBr(CO)2[NH(CH2CH2P(iPr)2)2]}`, complex 1. The Nature Communications borrowing-hydrogen study, [DOI 10.1038/ncomms12641](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms12641), identifies this precursor in its supporting methods and refers to its preparation in the JACS paper whose original crystallographic data are imported here. The full original crystallographic source and noncommercial data license are recorded in [THIRD_PARTY_LICENSE.md](THIRD_PARTY_LICENSE.md).

The verified formula is C18H37BrMnNO2P2, 62 atoms per molecule. The asymmetric unit contains two independent molecules, 124 atoms. Four explicit symmetry operations generate the 496-atom cell; periodic connectivity produces exactly eight finite 62-atom components, agreeing with Z = 8. Each molecule crosses a periodic boundary and has been unwrapped before extraction. No partial occupancy, disorder tag or nonzero periodic graph cycle was found. The reconstructed bond graph agrees with the CIF bond list; the largest bond-distance reconstruction discrepancy is 0.001499 Å, consistent with the recorded coordinate precision used here.

Both Mn sites have six detected bonded neighbors: two phosphorus donors, one nitrogen donor, two carbonyl carbons and one bromide. The explicit N-H distances are 0.835961 Å for Mn1 and 0.846499 Å for Mn2. These X-ray hydrogen positions were retained, not silently lengthened. The 150 K crystal refinement has mixed hydrogen treatment: 72 H sites in the asymmetric unit carry `calc` flags, while the two N-H sites carry `d` flags. An individual CIF site flag is not proof of a solution protonation state or DFT suitability.

The imported structure is a **precatalyst**, not the deprotonated active state in the earlier generated catalog. It has an NH-responsive PNP ligand and differs from the Fe hydroxypyridine O-H model used in the separate local SCF pilot. It has two CO ligands and a bound bromide, with no hydride in the exported precursor. Do not remove Br or H without defining the chemical transformation, charge balance, counterions and products.

Charge 0, formal Mn(I), d6 electron counting and multiplicity 1 are starting chemical assignments. The CIF importer has not experimentally verified the charge or spin ground state. The metadata therefore retain `chemistry_review_required=true`, `geometry_optimized=false`, `active_state_generated=false`, and the verification receipt retains `quantum_submission_ready=false`. A bridge may prepare offline singlet/triplet/quintet candidate inputs for review; their presence must not be presented as approved calculations, converged solutions or measured spin states.
